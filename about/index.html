001 / Who I Am

A student figuring things out, one day at a time.

I'm pursuing CA Inter alongside B.Com — and somewhere in between studying balance sheets and surviving exams, I decided to start making art. Not because I had training. Not because I was naturally gifted. But because of a quiet "what if I actually tried?"

I'm drawn to creativity in all its forms — digital art, design, technology, films, games, storytelling, and immersive experiences that make you feel something without quite knowing why.

I'm not a prodigy. I didn't grow up painting portraits or filling sketchbooks with masterpieces. This journey started from pure curiosity — and the desire to slowly, imperfectly, become better over time.

This website exists as an honest record of that.

"I care more about learning than about being good."
— something I keep reminding myself
002 / Why I Create

Creating is how I
process things.

Some emotions are too vague for words. Some ideas only become clear when you try to make something from them.

I'm not making art to impress anyone. I'm making art to understand myself better — to experiment without pressure, to document the messy, unfinished process of becoming.

I care more about what something makes me feel than whether it's technically correct. I care more about growing than being polished.

Art gives me a place to be imperfect on purpose — and that's deeply freeing.

003 / The Journey

This is not a story about arriving somewhere. It's a story about the road itself — the detours, the restarts, the days nothing gets made, and the quiet victories of showing up anyway.

I procrastinate.

Some weeks nothing gets made. I sit with an empty canvas, endless open tabs, and thoughts that never quite become pixels. It happens. A lot.

I get distracted.

CA Inter exists. B.Com exists. Films exist. Games exist. The internet exists. Creativity is always competing with everything — and it doesn't always win.

I show up anyway.

When I do create — even badly, even slowly — it feels like real progress. Small, quiet, imperfect. But forward. That's the whole point.

This website is a living document of that process —
not a highlight reel. A diary.
